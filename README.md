Live Nation is a global entertainment company that produces, promotes, and sells tickets for live events, including concerts, festivals, and theater productions. It operates a network of venues and partners with artists and brands to deliver experiences worldwide. The company also owns Ticketmaster, providing a comprehensive ticketing platform. Through its digital services, Live Nation offers fans access to event information, ticket purchasing, and exclusive content, while supporting artists with promotion and touring logistics.

**Areas:** Entertainment · Ticketing · Live Events · Music · Technology

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Live Nation: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
